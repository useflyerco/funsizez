About Me

Hi, I'm Charlie Stasiuk

I'm a web designer and UI builder based in Burlington, Ontario. I work on both halves of a website: how it looks and how it's built. The design, the layout and the interface you click through are all handled by one person, so nothing gets lost between the designer and the developer.

If you need a new site, a refresh of the one you have, or an interface built from a design you already love, I'd like to hear about it.

What I Do

Design and front-end build under one roof. Pick the piece you need, or have me take it from blank page to live site.

  • Web Design

    A site designed around your business and the people you want to reach.

    Learn More

    Web Design

    Every site starts with a conversation about what it needs to do. Maybe it's getting people to call you, showing off your work, or explaining what you offer. The design follows from that answer, not from a template.

    What's included

    • Page structure and navigation planned around your content

    • Layout, typography and colour that fit your brand

    • Designs for phone, tablet and desktop screens

    • Revisions with you before anything gets built

    Pricing depends on the size and scope of the site. Get in touch for a quote.

  • UI Building

    Turning designs into fast, working interfaces people can actually use.

    Learn More

    UI Building

    A good design only matters if it survives being built. I build interfaces in clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript that match the design closely and respond smoothly on every screen size.

    What's included

    • Building from my designs or from designs you already have

    • Responsive layouts that adapt to any screen

    • Interactions and animations that feel smooth, not flashy

    • Clean, readable code you or another developer can maintain

    Tell me about your project and I'll send a quote.

  • Responsive Builds

    Sites that look and work right on phones first, then everything bigger.

    Learn More

    Responsive Builds

    Plenty of people will see your site on a phone before they ever see it on a laptop. I design and build with small screens in mind from the start, so the mobile version isn't a squeezed-down afterthought.

    What's included

    • Layouts that reflow cleanly from phone to widescreen

    • Tap-friendly buttons, menus and forms

    • Images sized so pages stay quick to load

    • Testing across screen sizes before launch

    Ask about a responsive build.

  • Website Refresh

    A cleaner, more modern look for the site you already have.

    Learn More

    Website Refresh

    Sometimes a site doesn't need to start over. It needs to look current and be easier to use. A refresh keeps what's working and fixes what isn't.

    What's included

    • A review of your current site's layout and content

    • An updated visual design that still feels like you

    • Mobile improvements where the old site falls short

    • A rebuild of the pages that need it most

    Send me a link to your current site and I'll take a look.
