Hello from Burlington: why I build websites

Charlie StasiukWeb designer & UI builder, Burlington, ON
AboutHello from Burlington

I'm Charlie Stasiuk, a web designer and UI builder based in Burlington, Ontario. This blog is where I'll write about how I work and what goes into a good website, so you know what to expect before we ever talk.

What I do

I do two jobs that usually get split between two people.

  • Web design: how the site looks, how it's organized, and how it guides visitors to what they came for.
  • UI building: turning that design into a working site in code, one that responds properly on phones, tablets and desktops.

Why I do both

When one person designs a site and someone else builds it, things get lost in the handoff. Spacing drifts. An animation gets dropped because it was awkward to build. The mobile layout gets improvised because nobody drew it.

The person who designed your site should be the one who makes sure it gets built right.

Handling both halves means I can design things I know I can build well, and build things exactly the way they were designed. You also only have one person to talk to.

Why a blog?

Hiring someone to build your website is a big decision, and it's hard to judge a designer from a few portfolio images. Writing here lets you see how I think and how I work before you get in touch. I'll use it to:

  • Explain my process, step by step
  • Answer the questions people ask before starting a website
  • Share what I'm working on and learning

Working with someone local

Burlington sits on Lake Ontario between Hamilton and Oakville, in Halton Region. If your business is in the area, you'll be working with someone in your own time zone who knows the community you're trying to reach. If you're further away, that's fine too. We can handle everything over email.
