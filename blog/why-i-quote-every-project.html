Why I quote every project instead of listing prices

Charlie StasiukWeb designer & UI builder, Burlington, ON
PricingWhy I quote every project

You won't find a price list on my site, and I'm not hiding anything by leaving it off. A website can be one page or fifty, and any single number I posted would be wrong for most people reading it.

What changes the price

Two projects that are both called "a website" can be completely different amounts of work. The things that make the biggest difference are:

  • Number of pages. A one-page site and a ten-page site are different jobs.
  • Content. Whether your text and photos are ready, or need to be shaped as we go.
  • Scope. Design only, build only, or both.
  • Features. Contact forms, galleries, a blog, and other interactive pieces.
  • New or refresh. Updating an existing site is often a smaller job than starting from scratch.

A quote built around your project is fairer than a package built around an average one.

What a quote gets you

A price for your project specifically, with what's included spelled out. You'll know exactly what you're paying for before any work starts, so there are no surprises halfway through.

What to send me for an accurate quote

The more I know up front, the more accurate the quote. If you can, include:

  1. What your business does, in a sentence or two
  2. What you want the site to do: get calls, show your work, take enquiries
  3. Roughly how many pages you picture
  4. Links to a couple of websites you like
  5. Your current website, if you have one
  6. When you'd like it finished

Don't worry if you can't answer all of these. Send what you have and I'll ask about the rest.
