How a project with me works

Charlie StasiukWeb designer & UI builder, Burlington, ON
ProcessFrom first email to launch

If you've never had a website made, it's hard to know what the process looks like or what you'll be asked for. Here's how a project runs with me, from the first message to a live site.

The steps

  1. You get in touch. Tell me about your business, what you want the site to do, and roughly when you'd like it done. Links to sites you like are a big help.
  2. We talk it through. I'll ask questions to understand the scope: how many pages, what content you already have, and whether you need anything extra like contact forms or a gallery.
  3. You get a quote. It's based on your project, with what's included written out. Nothing starts until you're happy with it.
  4. Design. I design the key pages first so you can see the direction and give feedback before anything gets built.
  5. Build. Once the design is approved, I build the site in clean, responsive code and test it on phone, tablet and desktop screens.
  6. Review and launch. You check everything on a preview. We fix anything that isn't right, and then it goes live.

Nothing gets built until you've seen and approved the design.

What I'll need from you

The biggest thing that slows a website down is waiting on content. It helps a lot to have these ready, or at least in progress:

  • Your logo and brand colours, if you have them
  • The text for your pages, or notes I can work from
  • Photos you'd like to use
  • Feedback at each stage, especially on the design

Don't have all of that yet? That's normal. Tell me what you have and we'll work out the rest together.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come together. A single landing page and a multi-page business site are very different jobs. I'll give you a realistic timeline along with your quote, not a number picked to sound good.

Ready to start?

Step one is simply saying hello. Email charliecolestasiuk@gmail.com or use the contact form and tell me what you're working on.

Have a project in mind?

Every project is quoted individually. Tell me what you need.
